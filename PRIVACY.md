# Privacy Policy: The Holy Bible — Anno 1611

_Last updated 8 October 2026._

**Without an account**, nothing about you leaves your device. Bookmarks,
notes, reading place and settings are stored only on your phone or computer.

**With an account** (optional), we store your email address, a password
handled by our sign-in provider (Supabase; we never see it), and a copy of
your bookmarks, notes, reading place, chapters read and settings, so your
other devices can show them. Only you can read this data. It is not sold,
shared, or used for advertising or tracking.

**Other services** the app loads content from see your IP address, as any
website does: archive.org (narration and hymns), Wikimedia Commons
(engravings), Google Fonts (lettering), and GitHub (backup copy of the text).

**Deleting your data**: Account -> Delete my account removes your account and
everything stored with it. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling it removes
the copy on your device.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/Josh7895/kjv-1611-bible/issues.
