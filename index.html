      The Scripture text is bundled with this app (an online copy on GitHub is used only if the bundled one can't be read). Narration and the hymn playlist stream from archive.org, the engravings load from Wikimedia Commons, and the fonts from Google Fonts — those services see your IP address when they send these files, as any website does. The ambient drone is generated in your browser, and your settings and bookmarks are stored only on this device.
